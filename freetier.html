AWS Free account plans use eligible credits and restrict some services; paid plans can expose overage costs. Exact expiry and eligibility come from the account’s Billing and Settings view. The documented Free Tier path does not include multi-Region or cross-Region features. Do not ask participants to join an Organization, enable Control Tower or activate advanced features to follow the lab: account transitions can change their plan and access.
