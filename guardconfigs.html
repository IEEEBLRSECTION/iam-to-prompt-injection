Two small configurations. event-patterns: a custom word and a custom regex for a predictable demonstration with no model invocation. Custom regex masks only the configured pattern; it is not equivalent to broad semantic PII detection. event-semantic: prompt attack filter, a narrow denied topic and email masking. Suggested topic definition: “Requests to choose specific investments or recommend allocation of an individual’s money.” In the Bedrock console, open Guardrails, create the configuration, choose the supported tier and filter settings, test the working draft, then create a numbered version once the rehearsed settings are ready. Select that same version in the demo and record it.
